# Night desk coverage for hotels and clinics: the hours you cannot staff

A mother messages a clinic at 7:42pm. An assistant shortlists venues on Sunday. By morning the slot is taken elsewhere. You cannot staff every hour. You can stop losing the hours you cannot staff — if answers respect real calendars.

## Definition

**Unstaffed-hour leak** is demand that arrives when nobody can answer with accurate availability.

## What doing nothing already costs

| Leak | What you already pay |
|------|----------------------|
| No reply | Lost booking |
| Inaccurate reply | Double-book |
| Personal phone follow-up | History walks out |
| Inconsistent rates | Trust damage |

Escape, not shame: staff improvising were the system. Give them a calmer one.

## Framework

1. Map after-hours questions
2. Define what automation may confirm
3. Connect to live inventory
4. Handover sensitive cases with context
5. Review conversations weekly

## Believable operational scenario

Sunday event brief waits until Monday. The shortlist closes Sunday night. Coverage would have returned a qualified brief to sales.

## Common mistakes

- Bots without inventory
- Bots that diagnose or overpromise
- No handoff rules

## Fourteen-day path

Date the loss, define the object, capture new events, compare disputes, retire the shadow process for pilot scope. Simplify if calm does not improve.

## Why this leak compounds weekly

Improvisations multiply. New staff learn workarounds. Calm surfaces hide disputes until reconstruction is forced.

## What good looks like after two weeks

Faster capture, mid-week review, leave-safe records, leadership answers without calling three people.

## Metrics that matter

Time to answer, disputes, exception age, close or report length. Ignore vanity logins.

## Kinshasa / Nairobi notes

Mobile money, WhatsApp front door, patchy signal. Localise labels; keep object discipline.

## How AI changes this

AI reminds and summarises. It does not invent proof. Design evidence rules first.
